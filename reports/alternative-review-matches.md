# Alternative YouTube reviews

Checked the 43 games missing a Gameranx Before You Buy match: 29 verified alternative review links; 14 games still without a verified review.

Selected English-language game reviews from established publications and individual reviewers. Titles, channels and publication dates were verified against YouTube video metadata. Edition-specific reviews are identified in the table. Preview/trailer coverage and anime reviews were not substituted for game reviews. Solo Leveling uses a post-launch first-impressions review of the original ARISE game, not ARISE OVERDRIVE.

Applied to Supabase on 2026-09-14: 29 URLs saved, all 164 records verified. The previous 121 Gameranx matches were preserved.
